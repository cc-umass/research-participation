Research participation

EEG During Language Processing · UMass Amherst Linguistics

Thank you for your interest in participating in our study! You can sign up to our study in three easy steps.

  1. You first need to create your linguistics SONA account, if you do not already have one. To do so, click HERE. Click “Request Account.” Enter your information, and for the drop-down menu under “courses,” select the course you are earning credit for, or select “Non-course compensation” if you want to be paid. If you already have a linguistics SONA account, go to step (2).
  2. Once you have logged in, open the listing that matches how you want to be compensated:
    • EEG During Language Processing [PAID]
    • EEG During Language Processing [COURSE CREDIT]
    The session itself is the same either way.
  3. Find a time slot that works for you, and book it! If you cannot find a time slot that works for you, you can send us your availability (umasseeglab@gmail.com) — we may be able to accommodate you.

We are looking forward to seeing you in the lab! Our lab is located in the Integrative Learning Center (near the student union and the student center), and the room number is ILC N446.

Please do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns: umasseeglab@gmail.com.
